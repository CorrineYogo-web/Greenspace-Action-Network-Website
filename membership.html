Become a Member

Join a growing network of students, professionals, and organizations taking direct action on climate and community resilience across Kenya. Every membership fee funds real work on the ground.

Overview & Objectives

Why Membership Matters

GAN's membership structure fosters inclusive environmental action, youth empowerment, and strong community resilience across Kenya. Distinct entry points let students, grassroots advocates, young professionals, and institutions contribute directly to local climate solutions, each receiving benefits matched to their level of involvement.

Ksh 250 / year
Mazingira Supporter

For students, grassroots volunteers, and local community members.

Strategic FocusGrassroots Engagement & Inclusion building a broad base of active participants for field activities, tree planting, and awareness campaigns.
  • Digital membership badge & certificate
  • General newsletter & action alerts
  • Priority registration for tree-planting & cleanup drives
  • Discounted access to entry-level capacity workshops
Ksh 1,000 / year
Green Champion

For young professionals, environmental advocates, and researchers.

Strategic FocusCapacity Building & Advocacy governance participation, local working groups, and specialized skills through GAN's Climate Fellowship and policy workshops.
  • All Mazingira Supporter benefits
  • Access to Climate Fellowship training & mentorship
  • Voting rights in network general meetings
  • Access to GAN policy working groups & forums
  • Member spotlight feature in newsletters
Ksh 5,000 / year
Eco-Patron

For small businesses, CBOs, green startups, and corporate donors.

Strategic FocusPartnership & Organizational Sustainability CSR alignment, fundamental project funding, and institutional community leadership.
  • All Green Champion benefits
  • Logo placement on event materials & website
  • Certificate of Environmental Partnership / CSR recognition
  • Up to 3 designated delegate passes to GAN forums
  • Opportunity to co-host community restoration projects

"100% of membership fees directly fund community nursery development, grassroots climate workshops, and ongoing urban greenspace restoration projects across the network."
