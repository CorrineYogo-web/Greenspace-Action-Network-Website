About the Conference

What is GRSC2026?

The Greenspace Regional Sustainability Conference 2026 marks the 5th edition of GAN's flagship eco diplomacy convening. This action oriented platform brings together government leaders, corporate executives, young innovators, development partners, and civil society to advance scalable sustainability solutions across East Africa.

Unlike traditional climate conferences centered on discussion, the GRSC2026 focuses on action, partnerships, and resource mobilization. The conference bridges grassroots innovation with regional collaboration, shaping the next generation of climate leadership and green entrepreneurship.

Aligned with: Paris Agreement, African Union Agenda 2063, UN Agenda 2030, and COP31 Preparations

Conference Objectives

  • Enhance multi stakeholder participation in climate governance
  • Showcase youth led green enterprises and innovations
  • Strengthen cross sectoral partnerships
  • Mobilize financial resources for sustainability
  • Amplify youth voices in global climate negotiations

Partner Registration

Join us as an official partner and amplify your organization's impact. Partnerships are available at various engagement levels to suit organizational capacity and commitment.

Partnership Tiers

  • Platinum Partner: Event sponsorship, branding visibility, booth space, speaking slot
  • Gold Partner: Co-organizing support, branded booth, network recognition
  • Silver Partner: Booth space, conference materials, organizational profile
  • Bronze Partner: In-kind support, organizational listing, networking access

Exhibition Registration

Showcase your green innovations, products, and services to 500+ delegates from across East Africa. Exhibition booths are available on a first-come, first-served basis.

Booth Options

  • Standard Booth (3x3m): Ideal for most organizations | Limited interactive space
  • Premium Booth (6x4m): Enhanced visibility | Better for product demonstrations
  • Experiential Booth (Custom): Large-scale installations | Contact us for pricing

Research Paper Presentations

Submit your research findings, case studies, or innovations related to any of our 11 thematic areas. Selected presenters will have the opportunity to share their work during dedicated research sessions.

Submission Guidelines

  • Submit original or previously unpublished research
  • Presentation time: 15-20 minutes per presenter
  • Provide title, abstract (500 words max), and bio
  • Research must align with one or more thematic areas
  • All presenters will receive conference materials and networking access
